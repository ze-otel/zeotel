How we work

A clear process, from brief to handover.

Six stages, one project manager. You approve shop drawings, samples and a mock-up room before mass production starts.

Start with step 1
  1. 01

    Brief & design consultation

    We study your drawings, BOQ, brand standards and budget, and advise on materials and value engineering.

  2. 02

    Shop drawings

    Detailed production drawings for every item, sent for your approval before anything is cut.

  3. 03

    Samples & mock-up room

    You approve finishes, fabrics and a full mock-up room before mass production.

  4. 04

    Production in İnegöl

    Manufacturing in our factory, with quality checks at every stage and regular progress updates.

  5. 05

    Shipping & installation

    Export packing, logistics to site and installation by our own team.

  6. 06

    Handover & after-sales

    A final inspection with your team, then ongoing support for additions and replacements.
