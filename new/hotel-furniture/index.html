Questions

Hotel furniture FAQ

Can you manufacture from our drawings, BOQ and brand standards?

Yes. We study the drawings, BOQ, brand standards and budget, prepare shop drawings for every item and send them for your approval before anything is cut.

Do you build a mock-up room?

Yes. You approve finishes, fabrics and a full mock-up room before mass production.

Where is the furniture made?

In our own factory in İnegöl, Bursa, the heart of Turkey’s furniture industry. The project is managed from our head office in Istanbul.

Do you install on site?

Yes. We handle export packing, logistics to site and installation by our own team, followed by a final inspection with your team.

Can you also supply lighting, carpets and kitchen equipment?

Yes. Through approved partner suppliers we deliver chandeliers and lighting, carpets and curtains, greenery, gastronomy and kitchen equipment under the same contract.
