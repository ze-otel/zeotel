What to send for an accurate quotation

A complete request gets you a faster, more accurate quotation, priced line by line against your BOQ, and fewer surprises later.

  1. Bill of quantities (BOQ)

    Each item with code, dimensions, quantity per room type and finish.

  2. Drawings

    Plans and elevations as PDF or DWG, plus the FF&E schedule if you have one.

  3. Specifications and brand standards

    Materials, fabrics, finishes and any brand standards to follow.

  4. Project facts

    City, number of rooms and room types, target opening date and delivery address.

  5. Scope

    Supply only, or supply and installation, plus any partner items such as lighting, carpets or kitchens.

No BOQ yet? Send what you have: a mood board, reference photos and the number of rooms. We help you build the item list.
