Questions

What buyers ask us first

Is ZE-OTEL a hotel?

No. ZE-OTEL Hospitality Furniture is a manufacturer. We design, make, ship and install custom furniture (FF&E) for hotels, resorts, restaurants and serviced apartments, from our factory in İnegöl, Turkey.

Do you sell from stock?

No. Everything is made to order. Our catalogue shows pieces made for real hotels; we adapt dimensions, materials and finishes to your drawings, brand standards and budget.

What do I need to send for a quotation?

Drawings or a BOQ give the most accurate quotation. A mood board, a room count or even a photo of a style you like is enough to start.

Can we approve samples before production?

Yes. You approve finishes, fabrics and a full mock-up room before mass production starts.

Which countries do you deliver to?

We serve Turkey, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan, Lebanon and Libya, and have delivered projects in Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Lebanon, Palestine, Libya and Turkey. Export packing, shipping and installation are handled by our team.
